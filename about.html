Agencies and primes come to us for a working Atlassian platform their security team has reviewed.

Agencies and the primes who support them come to us to shorten the path from license to a working platform their security team has reviewed. Clovity Federal is the public sector practice of Clovity, Inc., an Atlassian Platinum Solution Partner.

Atlassian Platinum Solution Partner

Collaborative development platforms for Agile and DevSecOps teams

Clovity holds Atlassian's Platinum Solution Partner tier and builds apps for the Atlassian Marketplace. We implement and support Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management and Bitbucket for teams that plan, build and run government systems.

The roles stay clear. Atlassian owns the service authorization. Your agency owns its ATO. We configure the platform and document it so your assessors can trace each setting to a control.

  • Jira and Confluence for Agile delivery
  • Bitbucket and Jira for DevSecOps
  • Jira Service Management for ITSM
  • Government Cloud setup and documentation

Our mission: help civilian, defense, state and local teams reach their mission outcomes on an Atlassian platform they can defend in any security review.

Why federal agencies choose Clovity

Three things set the tone of every engagement, from the first briefing to steady-state support.

Secure environment expertise

Least-privilege permissions, SSO through your agency IdP and audit logging go in at setup, not after the first finding.

Federal compliance experience

We design around FedRAMP via Government Cloud, FISMA and NIST SP 800-53 control families, and write down every setting so your assessors can follow it.

Strategy through execution

One team from assessment and design through migration, licensing and ongoing support. No hand-offs for an auditor to find.

Markets we serve

Each sector answers to different rules and buys in a different way. We plan around both from day one.

Federal civilian

IT modernization, program management and service desks on Jira, Confluence and JSM. We design around FISMA, NIST SP 800-53 and FedRAMP via Atlassian Government Cloud.

Defense

Software delivery and acquisition programs using Jira and Bitbucket for DevSecOps. We'll tell you plainly where AGC stands for your impact level.

State & local

Resident services, public works projects and cross-department work. We plan around CJIS, StateRAMP or GovRAMP and state privacy rules where they apply.

Prime contractors

Integrators delivering for agencies and handling CUI. We help you meet NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC expectations in how Atlassian is set up.
