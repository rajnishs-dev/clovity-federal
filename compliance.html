Secure systems. Operations built for review.

We configure Atlassian around FedRAMP, FISMA and NIST SP 800-53, and document every setting.

We know the federal rules Atlassian work has to live under: FedRAMP, FISMA, NIST SP 800-53 and your agency's own security requirements. We configure Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management around them and document every setting, so your security team can check the work.

Compliance with clear scope

An authorization covers a specific service, not a brand. Atlassian Government Cloud holds FedRAMP Moderate for Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management. Atlassian's commercial cloud does not, and each Marketplace app is checked on its own. We set these lines at the start, so nobody assumes a control is covered when it isn't.

  • Atlassian runs the cloud service, its infrastructure and the FedRAMP authorization for Atlassian Government Cloud.
  • Your agency owns the ATO, the risk decisions, data categorization and who gets access.
  • Clovity Federal configures the platform, documents each setting and maps it to the controls in your security plan.
  • All of us share continuous monitoring. Atlassian watches the service. You and we watch access, configuration and change.

Federal compliance & security frameworks

These are the standards we design around. Each card says what we do and where our part stops. We hold none of these credentials ourselves, and we say so.

FedRAMP Moderate

Atlassian Government Cloud received FedRAMP Moderate authorization in March 2025 for Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management. We plan, stand up and configure it.

Not in scope: Clovity Federal holds no FedRAMP authorization. Commercial cloud and Marketplace apps sit outside AGC's authorization.

DISA impact levels: IL2, IL4 & IL5

As of Atlassian's March 2025 announcement, FedRAMP High and DoD IL5 were named as future goals, not current authorizations. We'll tell you plainly where AGC stands for your impact level.

Not in scope: We won't place data in AGC above the level it is authorized for.

FISMA & NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5

We map platform settings to the control families in your system security plan, so an assessor can trace a control to a real configuration.

Not in scope: We supply evidence. Your agency writes the SSP, accepts the risk and owns the ATO.

CMMC & NIST SP 800-171

For defense contractors and primes handling CUI, we design access, permission and audit settings that support your 800-171 practices.

Not in scope: We hold no CMMC certification and don't run assessments. Your assessor does.

CJIS

For state and local justice teams, we plan around CJIS Security Policy requirements with your security office: access, audit and where data may live.

Not in scope: Your agency decides what CJIS allows. We don't attest to it on anyone's behalf.

GovRAMP (formerly StateRAMP)

State programs that require GovRAMP get a plan built around it. We help you confirm the status of each product and app before anything goes live.

Not in scope: Clovity Federal holds no GovRAMP status. Product status comes from the provider's listing, not from us.

Section 508

Portals, request forms and page templates designed with accessibility in mind and reviewed for keyboard and screen reader use.

Not in scope: Atlassian owns accessibility of the core product. We cover what we build on top of it.

Zero Trust & ICAM

Identity-first setup in line with OMB M-22-09: SAML SSO through your agency identity provider for PIV/CAC sign-in, SCIM provisioning and least-privilege roles.

Not in scope: We connect to your identity provider. We don't issue credentials or run your IdP.

Hardened configurations, control by control

We build controls in from the first change, not the week before an audit. Four NIST SP 800-53 control families carry most of the weight in an Atlassian boundary. Here is how each one maps to the platform.

Least privilege, by design

Access is granted through groups and roles, never one person at a time. Admin power is split so no single account holds everything.

  • Jira permission schemes and project roles
  • Confluence space permissions and restrictions
  • Organization and product admin roles kept separate
  • IP allowlisting where your plan supports it

A trail your auditors can follow

Audit logs only help if someone keeps them and reads them. We set up both halves: retention and review.

  • Organization and product audit logs turned on and scoped
  • Log retention matched to your policy
  • Regular export to your log management or SIEM
  • A written review cadence with named owners

Change that ships with its controls

We record the baseline on day one. After that, every change follows the same approval path. Delivery keeps moving, and drift shows up in a review, not an incident.

  • Documented configuration baseline
  • Change log for schemes, workflows and settings
  • Vetting before any Marketplace app is installed
  • Data residency documented for your plan and hosting

Your identity provider stays in charge

Users sign in the way your agency already trusts. When someone leaves, their access goes with them.

  • SAML single sign-on through your agency IdP
  • PIV/CAC sign-in handled by your IdP
  • SCIM provisioning and deprovisioning with Atlassian Guard
  • Authentication policies for managed accounts

Governance built into every configuration

Control is what stays with you after we leave. Every engagement ends with written evidence your ISSO can file alongside the SSP, plus working sessions to walk your security staff through it.

How we support your ATO

Four phases. Each one ends with artifacts your ISSO and authorizing official can review.

  1. Assess

    Find your compliance needs, gaps and dependencies before anything is built.

    • Data categorization and impact level fit
    • Permission and access audit
    • App and integration risk inventory

  2. Architect

    Design the boundary and its controls around how your mission teams actually work.

    • Control mapping to NIST SP 800-53
    • Hosting option matched to your boundary
    • Risk register for your ISSO

  3. Operationalize

    Deploy the platform and its controls, and capture evidence as the work happens.

    • Hardened configuration and test runs
    • Configuration records and change log
    • Evidence package for your SSP

  4. Evolve

    Ongoing support that keeps the platform inside its approved baseline as it grows.

    • Periodic access reviews
    • Audit log checks for ConMon
    • Release and change tracking
