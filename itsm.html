Modern federal IT service management

Service desks on JSM where every request has an owner, an approval and a timestamp.

We build service desks on Jira Service Management where every incident, change and access request has an owner, an approval and a timestamp. JSM sits inside the FedRAMP Moderate authorization for Atlassian Government Cloud, alongside Jira and Confluence.

From legacy tools to one desk you can audit

Aging ticket tools, shared inboxes and spreadsheets lose the trail. We move that work into Jira Service Management and design it so the record of who asked, who approved and what changed is kept by default. Your agency owns the ATO. Atlassian owns the service authorization. We configure the desk and document it.

  • Legacy tool replacement. Request types, queues and history moved into JSM, with old records handled to your retention rules.
  • Workflow design. Approval steps placed where your policy says they belong, not where a tool default put them.
  • Process improvement. SLA and queue reports that show where requests stall, so you fix the process, not just the ticket.

How JSM supports federal ITSM

Six capabilities, each designed around a NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 control family. We write down how each one supports the control, so the configuration and the paperwork match.

Incidents

Severity rules, escalation paths and post-incident reviews that support your incident response (IR) procedures.

Service requests

One portal with forms that collect the right details up front and route each request to a named owner. Every step is logged for audit and accountability (AU).

Changes

Risk-based approvals, a change calendar and a recorded decision for every change. Built to support configuration management (CM) evidence.

Asset records

Hardware and software records tied to the tickets that change them, to help you meet inventory requirements under CM. We confirm which asset features your deployment includes before we design around them.

Knowledge

Self-service articles for staff, restricted Confluence spaces for internal runbooks. Permissions decide who sees what, in line with access control (AC).

Security requests

Account, access and exception requests in one logged flow: request, approve, provision, review. Maps to access control (AC) and periodic account reviews.

Solutions aligned to federal challenges

Three service patterns we build on Jira Service Management. Each one starts with your policy and ends with a record you can show an auditor.

Acquisition modernization

Transparency, speed, control.

An intake desk for purchase requests, with stages for funding, review and contracting office approval. Requesters see where their request stands. Approvers see the full history before they sign.

Workspace management

Where flexibility meets security.

Desk, room and space requests for hybrid teams, with facilities approval and physical access requests in the same flow. Security staff see who asked for access to which space, and when.

Asset and license tracking

Fewer duplicate purchases. Fewer lapsed licenses.

Hardware and software requests checked against what you already own, with renewal reminders set by automation. Each assignment and return is logged, so the record is ready when an auditor asks.

Operational coordination across every desk

Government service delivery goes beyond IT. Each desk gets its own project, permission scheme and request types. Staff see one portal. Agents see only their own queue.

HR service desk

Onboarding, offboarding and personnel questions, visible only to HR agents.

Facilities

Space, maintenance and physical access requests with a clear owner.

Security office

Badge, account and exception requests routed to security staff and logged.

FOIA & records intake

Intake dates captured on arrival, with retention planned around your NARA records schedule.

Finance and procurement

Purchase and invoice requests with approvals on the record.

Mission support

Program and field queues kept apart from back-office desks.
