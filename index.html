FedRAMP Moderate Government Cloud, with the scope spelled out

Atlassian Government Cloud (AGC) received FedRAMP Moderate authorization in March 2025 and is listed on the FedRAMP Marketplace. It covers Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management. The authorization does not extend to Atlassian's commercial cloud or to Marketplace apps, so each app is checked on its own.
