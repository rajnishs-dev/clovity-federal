Cloud upgrades with your data under control

Atlassian Data Center reaches end of life in March 2029, and legacy Server is already past end of support. We move you from Data Center to cloud with data governance and security controls planned in from the first inventory. Every export, copy and import is logged, rehearsed and signed off before production cutover.

  • Inventory, cleanup and data classification
  • Logged handling of exports and backups
  • Rehearsal migrations and UAT
  • Each app checked before it moves
  • Permissions rebuilt, not copied blindly
  • Cutover runbook and hypercare

Government Cloud's FedRAMP Moderate authorization covers Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management. It does not extend to Atlassian's commercial cloud or to Marketplace apps, so we check each app you rely on separately.

Atlassian license management, planned ahead

Existing-customer Data Center renewals end in March 2028. We help you compare Government Cloud with staying on Data Center for now, size licenses to real usage and line up renewals so no deadline forces a rushed choice.

  • License right-sizing
  • Government Cloud vs Data Center analysis
  • Renewal and co-term planning
  • Quotes for your contracting office

For procurement, we work with your contracting office and can share our UEI, CAGE code and current contract-vehicle details on request.

Implementation, integration and DevSecOps enablement

How we execute, in four steps

Every service follows the same path, with written decisions your contracting officer and ISSO can review at each step.

  1. Assess

    Find the gaps, friction points, compliance needs and dependencies in your current Atlassian setup.

  2. Architect

    Design the target platform around your mission workflows, with controls mapped to NIST SP 800-53 families.

  3. Operationalize

    Deploy the platform, pipelines and controls, then hand over configuration records and runbooks.

  4. Evolve

    Tune performance and resilience through ongoing support, and keep the baseline current as Atlassian ships changes.
