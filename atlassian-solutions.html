Ask our federal team
What are Atlassian federal solutions?

Atlassian products such as Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management, set up to meet government needs for access control, audit logging, records and procurement. For many agencies that means Atlassian Government Cloud. Others run Data Center while they plan their move.

How can Atlassian support U.S. federal agencies?

It gives program, IT and security teams one platform for planning, documentation and service delivery. When it is configured well, the same platform also keeps the records your assessors ask for: who had access, who approved a change and when.

Which Atlassian products fit federal IT modernization?

Jira for projects and programs, Confluence for policies and knowledge, and Jira Service Management for IT service delivery. These three run in Atlassian Government Cloud, inside its FedRAMP Moderate scope. For Bitbucket, confirm the hosting option for your data first.

What is Atlassian Government Cloud?

A cloud for U.S. government run separately from Atlassian's commercial cloud. It received FedRAMP Moderate authorization in March 2025 for Jira, Confluence and Jira Service Management. That authorization does not cover the commercial cloud or Marketplace apps. As of the March 2025 announcement, FedRAMP High and DoD IL5 were goals, not granted. More on Government Cloud.

Can Jira be used for federal project management?

Yes. Jira supports Agile and traditional delivery, portfolio planning and requirements traceability. We add project roles, required approvals and change history so the work holds up to review.

How does Jira Service Management support federal ITSM?

It covers requests, incidents, problems and changes in one service desk, with a self-service portal and SLAs. Approvals are captured on each record, so access and change decisions are easy to trace. See our ITSM work.

Can Atlassian help modernize legacy federal workflows?

Yes. We map how work runs today, keep what your rules require and rebuild the rest in Jira, Confluence or JSM. Legacy Server is already out of support and Data Center reaches end of life in March 2029, so many agencies pair this with a migration.

Does Clovity hold an ATO or FedRAMP authorization?

No. Atlassian holds the service authorization for Government Cloud, and your agency issues its own ATO. Our part is to configure the platform and document that configuration so your security team can review it.

How do we buy through Clovity?

We work with your contracting office on the route that suits your agency. We can share our UEI, CAGE and current contract-vehicle details on request.

What Atlassian services does Clovity provide?

Government Cloud readiness and setup, migrations, security configuration and documentation, ITSM on Jira Service Management, licensing, managed services, professional services, training and governed AI. All services.
